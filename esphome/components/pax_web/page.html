    Send your files

    Choose apps, ROMs, artwork or a folder. Folder uploads keep their directory structure under the destination you choose.

    Examples: /roms/pax for apps, /roms/nes for NES games, or /backgrounds for artwork.

    Paths use letters, numbers, dots, underscores and hyphens. Each file can be up to 1 GiB. Rename files with spaces or other characters before sending them.
